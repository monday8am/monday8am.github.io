Hold the Band

Nine short levels on how a cashless terminal handles a wristband tap. Press and hold a band to put it on the reader, and let go to take it away. Letting go at the wrong moment is half the game.

    What just happened

    The rules follow step in TapStateMachine.kt, TapRunner.kt and WristbandTaps.kt at 191d8c224, ported from the Wristband Tap Machine explorer. Use the explorer to see every transition and every operation. Simplified for teaching: amounts and tokens are made up, the screen is always ready, and the used-token rule is cashless's own. The SDK today reads a band even on a resume; the game leaves that ignored read out. Band C and its uid are invented for the damaged-band level.
